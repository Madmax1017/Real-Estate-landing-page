What Magma Offers?

Dataroom: Structured. Secure. Validated.

The Dataroom centralizes all building documentation into a single, permissioned environment. Every document is organized, validated, timestamped, and traceable.
• Eliminate fragmented files and email chains
• Accelerate due diligence and underwriting
• Strengthen compliance and audit readiness
• Control stakeholder access with clear permissions
• Create a reliable, finance-grade source of truth


BIM: From Static Model to Operational Intelligence.

Magma connects the BIM to real-world documentation and asset workflows. The 3D model becomes more than a design file, it becomes a living operational layer.
• Bridge the gap between design and operations
• Visualize equipment, systems, and documentation in context
• Improve maintenance, asset tracking, and decision-making
• Support ESG and lifecycle management


Scan: Reality capture, Verified.

Magma integrates on-site scans to align physical reality with documentation and BIM. What exists in the field matches what exists in the platform.
• Reduce discrepancies between plans and reality
• Improve renovation and retrofit accuracy
• Strengthen risk assessment and insurance workflows
• Enable remote inspections and transparency
• Maintain an up-to-date digital representation of the asset
